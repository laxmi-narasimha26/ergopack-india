About ErgoPack India

Benz Packaging Solutions, India's leading packaging innovation company, has partnered with ErgoPack Germany to bring revolutionary ergonomic pallet strapping technology to the Indian market.

ErgoPack's patented chain-lance system is the world's only technology that eliminates worker bending during pallet strapping. The strap travels under the pallet and rises to the operator at standing height—transforming warehouse safety and productivity.
