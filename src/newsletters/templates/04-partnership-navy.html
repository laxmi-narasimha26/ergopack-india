The Partnership Story

Benz Packaging Solutions, India's trusted packaging innovation leader, identified a critical gap: Indian warehouse workers were suffering preventable back injuries from decades-old strapping methods.

Meanwhile, in Germany, ErgoPack had already solved this problem with patented technology. The partnership was inevitable—bringing 50 years of German ergonomic engineering to transform Indian logistics.

The Journey

Germany • 1974

ErgoPack invents the world's first chain-lance strapping system—technology that routes the strap under the pallet and raises it to standing height.

India • 2024

Benz Packaging Solutions establishes exclusive partnership with ErgoPack Germany to serve the Indian market.

Launch • 2025

ErgoPack India officially launches with three product lines, bringing zero-bend strapping to Indian warehouses.
