How the Chain-Lance System Works

1. Positioning

Operator positions the machine at the pallet edge. No walking around required.

2. Chain-Lance Deployment

The patented lance travels beneath the pallet, carrying the strap to the opposite side.

3. Strap Return

The strap rises to standing height where the operator receives it without bending.
