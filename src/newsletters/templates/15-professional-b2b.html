About the Partnership

Benz Packaging Solutions, one of India's leading packaging companies, has partnered with ErgoPack Germany to bring patented ergonomic pallet strapping technology to the Indian market.

ErgoPack machines use a patented chain-lance system that threads the strapping beneath the pallet and brings it up to the operator at standing height. This eliminates the need for workers to bend down or walk around pallets.
