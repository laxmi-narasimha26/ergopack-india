The Problem with Traditional Strapping

Slow: Manual strapping takes approximately 120 seconds per pallet

Strain: Workers bend down 4+ times per pallet, causing back injuries

Inefficient: Workers must walk around each pallet to complete strapping

The ErgoPack Solution

Fast: ErgoPack completes strapping in under 40 seconds per pallet

Safe: Zero bending required. AGR-certified for back health

Efficient: Operator stays in one position. Chain-lance does the work
