What Makes ErgoPack Different?

ErgoPack uses a patented chain-lance mechanism that threads the strapping material beneath the pallet and brings it up to waist height. This means:

1

No bending down to reach beneath pallets

2

No walking around the pallet to complete strapping

3

Faster strapping — under 40 seconds vs 120 seconds manual
