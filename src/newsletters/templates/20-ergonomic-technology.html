About ErgoPack

ErgoPack Deutschland GmbH manufactures the world's only pallet strapping systems that eliminate worker bending. The patented chain-lance technology threads the strap beneath the pallet and delivers it to the operator at waist height.

Benz Packaging Solutions is the exclusive partner for ErgoPack in India, providing sales, installation, and service support nationwide.
