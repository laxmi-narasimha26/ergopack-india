What changed on 1 April 2026

India's four new Labour Codes are now in uniform operation, and the statutory wage floor is being revised upward — the twice-yearly VDA revision applies automatically. For any floor that still straps pallets by hand, that step's cost is no longer flat; it climbs with every revision. Manual strapping ties up two operators per pallet. A mobile ErgoPack does it with one, in under 40 seconds — taking that task off the wage bill for good.
