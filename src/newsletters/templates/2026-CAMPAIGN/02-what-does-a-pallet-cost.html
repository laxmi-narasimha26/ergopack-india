The "manual is free" myth

"We already have the people, so manual strapping costs us nothing." It feels true — and it's the most expensive assumption on the dock. The three costs above aren't a slogan; they're real, recurring line items spread across your books. Here's what each one actually does to your numbers.
1

Two people, ~120 seconds — every pallet

Hand-strapping is a two-person job that repeats every shift. After 1 April 2026 that hour costs more, and it climbs at the next revision.
2

Wasted strap — ~12% on every load

Tension by feel over-applies strap. Across a year of pallets, that over-use is a line item you're paying without seeing it.
3

The pallet that fails in transit

Inconsistent tension is the loose load that shifts, gets damaged or rejected. One rejected export container can outweigh a year of "savings."

Add the three together and the real number for a typical floor is around ₹25 lakh a year — recovered by a mobile machine in 6–18 months. But you shouldn't take our typical floor; you should run yours.
