The root cause

Hand-strapping applies tension by feel, so it varies pallet to pallet. The loosest pallet is the one that settles, shifts and collapses at sea — and gets the consignment rejected. It isn't bad luck; it's variability.

Automated securing applies the same calibrated tension to every pallet — up to 2500N, sealless friction weld, rust-free PET that survives weeks of container humidity. Consistency replaces guesswork, and the loose-pallet failure simply goes away.
