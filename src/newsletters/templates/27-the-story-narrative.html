Chapter One

The Problem No One Was Solving

In warehouses around the world, workers were bending down hundreds of times per shift to strap pallets. The toll on their bodies was devastating—chronic back pain, herniated discs, and careers cut short by injury.

In Germany, a team of engineers saw this problem and asked a simple question: "What if we could bring the strap to the worker, instead of the worker to the strap?"

🇩🇪 Germany, 1980s

Chapter Two

The Birth of ChainLance

The answer came in the form of a revolutionary patented technology: the ChainLance. A mechanism that threads strapping material beneath the pallet and brings it up to waist height—allowing workers to complete the entire strapping process while standing upright.

The results were remarkable. Back injuries plummeted. Productivity soared. Worker satisfaction reached new heights. ErgoPack had solved a problem that had plagued industries for generations.

🌍 55+ Countries, 10,000+ Systems

Chapter Three

The Journey to India

As ErgoPack expanded across the globe, one of the world's largest industrial markets awaited: India. With its rapidly growing manufacturing sector and millions of warehouse workers, the need for ergonomic solutions was immense.
