German Industrial Leader
Enters Indian Market

ErgoPack Deutschland GmbH partners exclusively with Benz Packaging Solutions
to bring ergonomic pallet strapping technology to India

Industry Correspondent | Delhi NCR

In a significant move signaling major investment in the Indian manufacturing sector, German industrial equipment manufacturer ErgoPack Deutschland GmbH has announced an exclusive distribution partnership with Benz Packaging Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

The partnership brings ErgoPack's patented ergonomic pallet strapping systems to the Indian market, marking the company's strategic entry into one of the world's fastest-growing industrial economies.

"This partnership represents a significant milestone in making world-class ergonomic solutions accessible to Indian industries."

— Joint Statement

ErgoPack's technology eliminates the need for workers to bend when strapping pallets—a breakthrough innovation that has already been adopted by over 10,000 facilities across 55 countries worldwide. The patented ChainLance mechanism threads strapping material beneath pallets and brings it to waist height, allowing workers to complete operations while standing upright.
