Where pallets fail first (and how to prevent it)

If your outbound is high-value, high-volume, or export-grade, the risk is rarely obvious until a pallet shifts in transit. Here are the five failure points we see most often.

01. Tension variability

What it causes: micro-movement, settling, and gradual loosening under vibration.

What good looks like: controlled, repeatable tension settings — consistent every shift.

02. Strap placement drift

What it causes: edge crush, damaged cartons, and unstable stack geometry.

What good looks like: a defined strap path per pallet type, executed precisely.

03. Manual threading under the pallet

What it causes: shortcuts under pressure — leading to missed straps and uneven results.

What good looks like: automated strap feeding under and around the pallet.

04. Seal inconsistency

What it causes: weak joins that fail during handling or vibration.

What good looks like: repeatable sealing quality with stable parameters.

05. No standard across lines/sites

What it causes: unpredictable outcomes and constant firefighting.

What good looks like: a deployable SOP — one operating standard, everywhere.

BENZ takeaway: If you have even 1–2 of these points, we can diagnose the root cause in a short call and recommend a system-level fix (not a temporary workaround).
