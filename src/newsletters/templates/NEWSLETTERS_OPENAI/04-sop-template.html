The fastest way to improve pallet integrity is to standardize the final checkpoint. Here's a ready-to-use SOP skeleton your team can adopt today.

SOP TEMPLATE (EDIT THE [BRACKETS])

Title: Pallet Strapping SOP – [SITE/LINE]
Applies to: [PRODUCT/SKU GROUP], [PALLET TYPE]
Strap spec: [PP/PET], [WIDTH], [THICKNESS]
Pattern: [2 straps / 3 straps / cross], [TOP/ SIDE]
Tension setting: [LOW/MED/HIGH] / [VALUE IF KNOWN]

Steps
1) Verify pallet condition & load alignment.
2) Confirm strap spec + correct coil loaded.
3) Apply strap 1 at [POSITION].
4) Apply strap 2 at [POSITION].
5) If required, apply strap 3 at [POSITION].
6) Inspect seal quality (no partial weld / no misalignment).
7) Check strap placement matches the pattern & edge protection is in place.
8) Mark as cleared for dispatch.

QC checks (per shift)
- Random audit: [X pallets] for placement + seal quality.
- Incident log: record any strap/seal deviations & corrective action.
