A practical 30-day rollout plan

This is how we take you from evaluation to a stable, repeatable process — without disruption.

Week-by-week

Week 1 — Assessment & selection

BENZ maps your pallet types, strap spec, throughput, and constraints to select the right line (E-conomy / GO / X-pert).

Deliverable: recommended model + strap pattern + pilot plan.

Week 2 — Pilot demonstration

We validate performance on your real loads: placement repeatability, tension stability, seal quality, and operating flow.

Deliverable: confirmed settings + draft SOP.

Week 3 — Deployment readiness

Installation planning, operator training, SOP finalization, and preventive maintenance readiness (spares planning).

Deliverable: go-live checklist + service readiness plan.

Week 4 — Stabilize & standardize

We lock the operating standard: defined patterns, shift-level checks, and escalation rules so the process stays consistent.

Deliverable: a repeatable strapping standard (ready to scale to other lines/sites).

Why this comes from BENZ: We don't just introduce the technology. We own the rollout locally in India — application guidance, deployment coordination, and after-sales planning.
