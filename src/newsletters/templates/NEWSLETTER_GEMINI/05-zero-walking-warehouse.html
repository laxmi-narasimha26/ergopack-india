The Movement Waste No One Talks About

Fixed strapping machines create a hidden inefficiency: You have to move every single pallet to the machine, strap it, and move it back.

❌ The Fixed Machine Workflow
1. Forklift picks up pallet from assembly line
2. Transport to strapping station (30-60 seconds)
3. Position pallet (20 seconds)
4. Strap (30-60 seconds)
5. Transport to staging area (30-60 seconds)
Total: 2-3.5 minutes per pallet

Now multiply that by 500-1000 pallets per day. You're spending 25-60 hours per day just moving pallets around.
